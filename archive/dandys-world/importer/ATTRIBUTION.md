# Wiki discrepancy excerpts

`source_reviews.json` includes selected source excerpts by the Dandy's World Wiki
contributors, distributed and adapted under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
This applies to the wiki text and its adaptations, not the original importer code.
The rules select excerpts and add discrepancy annotations; they do not correct
the original articles. Revision IDs are pinned in each rule.
